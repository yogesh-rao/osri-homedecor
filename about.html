About OSRI

Hi, I'm Anita — the founder of OSRI. I started this brand from Bangalore with a simple idea: home decor should feel personal, not mass-produced. After years of hunting for pieces that actually had character — hand-tasseled throws, embroidered cushions, woven placemats — instead of settling for whatever showed up in a generic catalog, I decided to build the place I kept wishing existed. OSRI is that place.

Every OSRI piece — from sofa covers with hand-tied tassels to embroidered cushion covers, jute placemats and wooden crockery — is sourced directly from artisans and small workshops who still make things by hand. Working closely with weavers and craftspeople means every order supports real livelihoods, not just a supply chain, and it means no two pieces are ever quite identical.

We're based out of Electronic City, Bangalore, and ship across India. Whether you're setting up your first apartment or slowly re-doing a home you've lived in for years, I want OSRI to be the place you come back to for the small details that make a space feel like yours. Same-day delivery is available within Bangalore, and every order outside the city goes out through trusted courier partners.
