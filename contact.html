Common questions

Where is my order?
Tracking is emailed when the parcel leaves us. If it has been more than four working days since that email, send us the order number.
Can I return something?
Within seven days of delivery, unused and in its original packing. Full terms are on the policies page.
Do you take bulk or trade orders?
Yes — for hotels, homestays, gifting and resale. Tell us quantities and rough timelines and we will send pricing.
